# Made by Humans — Competitor Benchmark & Website QA

## Purpose
This document is a permanent reference for website, product and brand decisions.

The benchmark is not used to copy competitors. It is used to understand which trust mechanisms users already expect from serious certification systems and where Made by Humans must differentiate.

## Business-plan north star
Made by Humans is intended to become a cross-industry trust mark and standard for physical products whose material manufacturing steps are demonstrably performed by humans.

The long-term moat is not audit software or audit hours alone. It is the combination of:
- brand
- standard
- certificate base
- audit history
- public register
- trade / retail integration
- trusted network of auditors or certification bodies

Pilot first. Scale only after real audits, willingness to pay and consumer understanding are proven.

## Benchmark set

### Nest Verified Handcrafted — direct benchmark
Relevant strengths:
- precise definition of handcrafted
- validation of production techniques
- product / SKU mapping
- consumer-facing verified mark
- third-party assurance

Do not copy:
- artisan / cultural / social-impact positioning
- technique-specific craft taxonomy as the core brand narrative

Made by Humans difference:
- broader physical-product end-product standard
- not limited to classic handcraft
- technology-neutral
- focused on whether essential manufacturing steps are materially human-performed

### TÜV Rheinland / Certipedia
Relevant strengths:
- unique ID on the mark
- QR / ID links to public certificate information
- public explanation of test criteria
- mark-use rules
- misuse controls

Made by Humans should match or exceed:
- instant public verification
- clear status and validity
- exact scope of what was verified

### UL / UL Verify
Relevant strengths:
- the mark is tied to a precise verified claim
- unique identifier
- public verification database
- published mark usage rules
- follow-up / ongoing compliance concepts

Made by Humans should match or exceed:
- one exact consumer-understandable certified statement
- public evidence context
- lifecycle / status history

### OEKO-TEX
Relevant strengths:
- label check is extremely easy to find and use
- standards are explained in consumer language
- QR / label number verification
- strict labelling guidance
- buying / product directory

Made by Humans should learn:
- verification must be a primary navigation function
- mark communication must be standardized
- retailers and consumers should not invent their own wording around the mark

### FSC
Relevant strengths:
- public certificate and licence search
- filtering by status, certification body and geography
- published standards and procedures
- separation between scheme and certification bodies

Made by Humans should learn:
- public data should become deeper as the scheme scales
- long-term certification-body model must be structurally possible

### DNV / SGS / Bureau Veritas
Relevant strengths:
- institutional tone
- audit journey and digital customer services
- accreditation / impartiality / technical competence
- clear separation of certification from marketing language

Do not copy:
- massive service catalogues
- generic corporate claims
- complexity that hides the actual certified property

## Website architecture that Made by Humans should own

### Home
Must answer within seconds:
1. What is being certified?
2. What exact statement does the mark make?
3. How is the statement verified?
4. Where can the public check it?
5. How can a manufacturer enter the pilot?

### Standard
Must show:
- scope
- audit gates
- real positive and negative edge cases
- versioning
- governance principles
- status of the standard

### Manufacturer journey
Must show:
- who is suitable
- pilot scope
- audit journey
- evidence expectations
- no premature certification promise

### Public Register
Must make searchable:
- certification ID
- manufacturer
- product
- status

Later scale fields may include:
- standard version
- certificate body / auditor
- country / production site
- product family
- valid-from / valid-until
- status history

### Certificate record
Must show:
- exact certified statement
- product
- manufacturer
- standard
- issue date
- validity
- status
- public history
- visible warnings for suspension / revocation

## Future trust features to build when operationally ready
- QR on the final mark
- immutable / auditable status history
- complaint and mark-misuse reporting
- mark-use / communication guide
- standard document archive and changelog
- renewal and re-audit logic
- change-notification requirements for manufacturers
- auditor / certification-body field
- retailer and PIM / commerce integrations
- manufacturer portal only when pilot data justifies it

## Content QA — reject any public text that fails one of these
- Does it describe something that is actually true today?
- Does it match the business plan?
- Does it describe the certified property rather than vague values?
- Would a lawyer, auditor or journalist understand exactly what is meant?
- Does it avoid generic AI / agency slogan syntax?
- Does it avoid implying accreditation, independence or scale that does not yet exist?
- Is it understandable to a buyer without reading the full standard?

## Visual QA
- no AI-generated documentary imagery
- no generic smiling stock people
- no eco / handmade visual clichés
- no decorative numbering without informational purpose
- no oversized blocks merely to fill the page
- no gradients or 3D visuals
- no continuous logo rotation
- strict grid and quiet spacing
- seal is a functional mark, not decorative animation
- authentic production photography only when provenance and rights are clear
