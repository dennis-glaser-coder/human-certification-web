# Made by Humans — Photography Brief

## Principle
The photography must document real human manufacturing. It must never simulate authenticity.

## Source priority
1. Real pilot manufacturers and their actual production sites
2. Real partner companies and real production processes
3. Commissioned documentary photography in a real workplace
4. Exceptional documentary stock only if provenance and usage rights are clear

AI-generated imagery is not permitted for the public website.

## What to photograph
- material becoming a product through human work
- machine-assisted production where a person actively performs the product-defining step
- joining, sewing, shaping, finishing, welding, sanding, assembly
- quality inspection and measurement
- production documentation and traceability
- close details of hands, tools and material only when the wider process is also documented
- both manufactory and technically advanced production

## What not to photograph
- posed teams looking at a machine
- staged handshakes
- generic smiling employees
- "artisan lifestyle" clichés
- rustic eco imagery used as a shortcut for authenticity
- a person merely standing beside automation
- fake laboratory or audit situations
- anything generated or materially altered to invent a production reality

## Visual treatment
- documentary, observational, calm
- available light where possible
- natural skin tones and material colors
- restrained grading
- no cinematic teal/orange look
- no artificial depth-of-field gimmicks
- no excessive retouching
- no fake sparks, steam, dust or dramatic effects

## Minimum metadata for every website image
- company / production site
- city / country
- real process shown
- photographer / source
- usage rights
- capture date
- confirmation that the depicted process is real and not staged to misrepresent certification criteria

## Recommended first shoot
A first pilot manufacturer should yield:
- 1 wide production environment
- 2 people-at-process images
- 2 detail images of material transformation
- 1 machine-assisted human process
- 1 quality-control image
- 1 traceability/documentation image
- 1 finished product in production context

The goal is not advertising beauty. The goal is credible evidence of real human value creation.
